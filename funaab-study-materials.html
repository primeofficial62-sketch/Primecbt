Study materials inside PRIME CBT

The Materials section lets signed-in students open course notes and PDF documents inside the platform. If a page is hard to follow, you can ask for an AI explanation of that page without leaving the document.

Materials are available to signed-in users and are not downloadable from this page. The list of available materials depends on the course and can change over time.

Getting more from your materials

  • Skim headings first, then read in short focused blocks with breaks.
  • Write a one-line summary of each topic and revise those lines daily.
  • Explain a topic aloud in your own words; if you cannot, revisit it.
  • Test yourself with CBT practice after reading, rather than rereading again.
