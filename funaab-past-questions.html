Practice questions for your courses

PRIME CBT lets you answer practice questions course by course and topic by topic. After each session you can see what you got right, what you missed and which topics need more work.

The questions on the platform are practice material for revision. They are not official university papers, and we do not promise that any question will appear in an examination.

How to use practice questions well

  • Start with a topic you find hard, not the one you already know.
  • Attempt before you check. Recalling an answer helps it stick more than rereading.
  • Study your mistakes. Note why an option was wrong.
  • Repeat later. Return to the same topic a few days on to see whether it has improved.
  • Add timed sessions as your exam gets closer.
