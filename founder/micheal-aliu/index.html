Why I Built PRIME CBT

PRIME CBT was created from an interest in making academic preparation more accessible, convenient and effective for university students.

Students often need to search through different sources to find past questions, relevant course materials and opportunities to practise for computer-based examinations. Having these resources scattered across different platforms can make preparation unnecessarily difficult.

"I believe students should have access to better digital tools that make learning easier, improve preparation and help them approach examinations with greater confidence."

This thinking inspired the development of PRIME CBT, beginning with students of the Federal University of Agriculture, Abeokuta (FUNAAB).

The platform brings together examination practice, academic resources and smarter learning experiences in one place. Its broader vision is to make useful educational technology accessible to more students across Nigeria.

PRIME CBT – Educational Technology

PRIME CBT is an educational technology project founded and developed by Micheal Aliu. It is designed to support university students through digital academic tools.

What the platform offers

  • Computer-based test practice and examination preparation.
  • Access to academic and course learning materials.
  • Interactive practice experiences.
  • Learning support through AI-assisted features.
  • Student engagement and community-oriented features.
  • Progress tracking and performance-oriented learning experiences.

PRIME CBT began with a focus on FUNAAB students, with a vision of expanding educational opportunities and digital examination preparation to other institutions.
