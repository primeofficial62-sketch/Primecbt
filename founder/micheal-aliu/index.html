Biography

Micheal Aliu (Micheal Imodu Aliu) is a Nigerian digital entrepreneur, web developer, brand strategist and social media manager based in Ibadan, Oyo State.

He is the founder of PRIME CBT, an educational technology platform designed to improve how university students prepare for examinations, practise questions and access academic learning resources.

His professional interests sit at the intersection of technology, creativity, digital communication and entrepreneurship. He believes technology should solve real problems and make everyday activities easier.

Why PRIME CBT Was Built

PRIME CBT came from an interest in making academic preparation more accessible, convenient and effective for university students.

Students often have to search through different sources to find past questions, course materials and ways to practise for computer-based examinations. Having these resources scattered across platforms can make preparation unnecessarily difficult.

The idea behind PRIME CBT is simple: give students better digital tools that make learning easier, improve preparation and help them approach examinations with greater confidence.

This thinking led to the development of PRIME CBT, beginning with students of the Federal University of Agriculture, Abeokuta (FUNAAB). The platform brings examination practice, academic resources and smarter learning experiences together in one place, with a longer-term aim of reaching more students across Nigeria.

PRIME CBT – Educational Technology

PRIME CBT is an educational technology project founded and developed by Micheal Aliu. He leads the product and its development, including the website, student accounts and learning features.

What the platform offers

  • Computer-based test practice and examination preparation.
  • Access to academic and course learning materials.
  • Interactive practice experiences.
  • Learning support through AI-assisted features.
  • Student engagement and community-oriented features.
  • Progress tracking and performance-oriented learning experiences.

PRIME CBT began with a focus on FUNAAB students, with a vision of expanding digital examination preparation to other institutions.
