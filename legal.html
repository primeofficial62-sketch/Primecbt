Privacy Policy

PRIME-CBT ("we", "us") helps students prepare for exams. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. By using PRIME-CBT you agree to it.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your full name, email address and password (stored securely, never in plain text).
  • Study profile: school, department, level and, optionally, your matric number.
  • Google sign-in: if you use it, we receive your name and email from Google. We do not see your Google password.
  • Usage data: questions you attempt, scores, topic progress, streaks, XP and leaderboard standing.
  • Technical data: basic device and browser information, used to keep the app working and secure.

2. How we use it

  • To create and secure your account, and send verification and password-reset codes.
  • To show your progress, weak topics and readiness score.
  • To show leaderboards (your name and score may be visible to other students in your department or class).
  • To improve questions, features and performance, and to prevent abuse.

3. AI explanations

When you ask for an AI explanation of a PDF page, the text of that page may be sent to an AI service to generate the answer. Please do not request explanations for material that contains private personal information.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We use trusted service providers for authentication, database hosting, website hosting and AI features. They process data only to provide those services. We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Storage and security

Your data is stored with our hosting providers and protected with industry-standard safeguards. No online service can be 100% secure, so please choose a strong password and keep it private. Your browser also stores small items (such as a login session and your sign-up progress) on your device.

6. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and similar laws, you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Email support@prime-cbt.com and we will respond within a reasonable time. Deleting your account removes your profile and progress, except where we must keep records by law.

7. Children

PRIME-CBT is meant for university and college students. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. When we make important changes we will update the date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.

9. Contact

Questions? Email support@prime-cbt.com.
