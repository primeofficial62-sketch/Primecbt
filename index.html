About PRIME CBT

Education deserves a smarter approach.

PRIME CBT is an educational technology platform created to make learning, academic preparation and examination practice more accessible. We bring together university learning resources and digital academic tools in one accessible environment.

From university students preparing for semester examinations to candidates working towards their next academic opportunity, PRIME CBT is built to support every step of the learning journey.
