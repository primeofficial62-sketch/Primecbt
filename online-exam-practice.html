What is online examination practice?

Online examination practice means answering exam-style questions on a screen, usually with a timer, so you rehearse both the content and the format of a computer-based test. It works well alongside reading and notes because it makes you recall information instead of just reviewing it.

Benefits of practising online

Familiar format

Get comfortable with on-screen questions, options and navigation before the real thing.

Immediate feedback

Find out straight away which answers were right and why.

Flexible

Practise on a phone or computer, in short sessions that fit your day.

Measurable

Your results show which topics are improving and which are not.

A simple practice routine

  • Pick one course and one topic.
  • Do a short untimed set and review every mistake.
  • Repeat the topic a few days later.
  • Move to timed sets as your exam date nears.
  • Use the exam countdown and study streak to stay consistent.

Frequently asked questions

Does practising online guarantee a pass?

No tool can guarantee results. Regular practice and good revision habits improve your preparation.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. PRIME CBT works in a mobile browser and can be installed to your home screen on supported devices.
