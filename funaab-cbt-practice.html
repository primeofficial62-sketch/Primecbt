Why practise in a CBT format?

Knowing a topic and answering questions on a screen against a clock are different skills. Practising in a CBT-style environment helps you get used to reading questions carefully, choosing between close options and managing your time, so the format itself is not a surprise.

What you can do on PRIME CBT

Choose a course, then work on one topic at a time instead of repeating whole papers.

Timed practice

Use the timer to build a steady pace and learn how long you spend per question.

Instant feedback

See how you did straight after answering and review the questions you got wrong.

Track weak topics

Your progress page shows where your accuracy is lower so you know what to revise next.

Study streaks and countdown

Set your exam date for a countdown and keep a daily streak going.

How to prepare for a computer-based test

  • Practise little and often rather than cramming the night before.
  • Read every option before choosing; some answers are almost right.
  • Skip and flag hard questions, then return with the time you saved.
  • Review your wrong answers, not just your score.
  • Move to timed practice as your exam gets closer.
