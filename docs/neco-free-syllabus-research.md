# NECO Free Syllabus Research — 2026-11-27

## Finding

A complete, free, official NECO SSCE syllabus was not located. NECO's 2027 SSCE Internal page states that the syllabus costs ₦2,000 per copy. The current registration guidelines repeat this price. Free timetables and registration guides are not subject syllabuses.

## Verified first-party sources

1. NECO SSCE Internal page: https://neco.gov.ng/exams/ssce-internal
2. NECO 2027 SSCE Internal Registration Guidelines: https://neco.gov.ng/2027%20SSCE%20INTERNAL%20REGISTRATION%20GUIDELINES.pdf
3. NERDC revised Senior Secondary Education Curriculum: https://www.nerdc.gov.ng/content_manager/new_senior_curriculum_home.html
4. NERDC public Curriculum View: https://nerdc.org.ng/CurriculumView.aspx
5. NERDC public Subject Listings: https://nerdc.org.ng/SubjectListings.aspx
6. Federal Ministry of Education / NERDC approved subject list notice: https://education.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/FME-APPROVED-GUIDELINES.pdf

## What can be used for free

NERDC's public portal exposes senior-secondary subject listings and selected topic/theme views. For example, its General Mathematics view exposes Number and Numeration, Algebraic Process, Geometry, and Statistics themes. These are curriculum references, not proof of NECO's current examinable syllabus.

NERDC's e-Curriculum portal says posted syllabi, lecture recordings, slides, and other educational materials are for personal study. Public access does not grant PRIME-CBT a right to bulk-copy or commercially republish the material.

## PRIME-CBT ingestion decision

- Record NERDC as a separate curriculum_reference source, never as a NECO-issued source.
- Store publisher, exact source URL, retrieval date, curriculum edition, access/rights note, and review status.
- Use public subject/topic labels only as provisional research references until an educator verifies NECO alignment and reuse rights are clarified.
- Do not seed copied NERDC topic descriptions or full curriculum documents into the student-facing question bank.
- Keep NECO syllabus status awaiting_authorised_copy_or_permission.
- Keep questions marked official_past_question only when their provenance and reuse rights are established; otherwise create independently authored original_practice questions and label them accordingly.
