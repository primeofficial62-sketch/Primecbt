About PRIME CBT

PRIME CBT is an educational technology platform built to make examination preparation easier, starting with students at FUNAAB and growing toward institutions across Nigeria.

What is PRIME CBT?

PRIME CBT is an educational technology (EdTech) platform that helps university students prepare for examinations through accessible digital tools. It brings computer-based test (CBT) practice, academic course materials, AI-powered learning assistance and progress tracking into one place that works on a phone or a computer.

The slogan says it plainly: Prepare. Practise. Pass.

The problem we set out to solve

Preparing for exams is harder when study resources are scattered across chat groups and loose files, and when there are few chances to practise in the format of a real computer-based test. Students also often cannot tell which topics are holding their results back until it is too late to fix them.

PRIME CBT was created to put practice, materials and feedback in one place, so that preparation is more organised and easier to keep up.

Who PRIME CBT is for

PRIME CBT is built first for students of the Federal University of Agriculture, Abeokuta (FUNAAB). Our vision is to serve students at other universities, colleges and educational institutions across Nigeria over time.

PRIME CBT is an independent educational technology platform. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or accredited by the Federal University of Agriculture, Abeokuta (FUNAAB) or any other institution.

What the platform offers

CBT practice

Answer practice questions in a CBT-style screen with a timer and instant feedback, and drill one topic at a time.

Course materials

Open notes and PDF materials inside the platform and ask the AI assistant to explain a page you find confusing.

AI learning assistance

Ask questions and get simple explanations of topics you are studying.

Progress tracking

See your accuracy, weak topics and study streak update as you practise.

Student features

Community and leaderboard features, an exam countdown and focus sessions help you stay consistent.

Works on your phone

Use PRIME CBT in your browser, or install it to your home screen on supported devices.

Our mission

To make academic learning and examination preparation easier and more accessible for students through practical digital learning tools.

Our vision

A future in which students at universities, colleges and other institutions across Nigeria can prepare for their examinations with confidence, starting with FUNAAB.

Our values

Accessibility

Learning tools should be easy to reach, including on a phone.

Honesty

We describe what the platform does and does not do, and we do not make claims we cannot support.

Student focus

Features exist to help students prepare, practise and improve.

Continuous improvement

We keep improving the platform as we learn from how students use it.

Meet the founder

Micheal Aliu is the founder of PRIME CBT. He started the platform to make examination preparation more accessible and better organised for students, beginning with the FUNAAB community.

Commitment to accessible education and digital innovation

We believe good preparation tools should not be out of reach. PRIME CBT is built to be simple to use on common devices, and we keep refining it with digital tools such as AI-assisted explanations that support, rather than replace, a student's own study.

Where we are going

PRIME CBT begins with FUNAAB students. As the platform grows, we aim to support other universities, colleges and educational institutions across Nigeria. Any institutional partnership will be announced only once it is confirmed.
