## Short biography (about 60 words)
PRIME CBT is an EdTech platform founded by Micheal Aliu. It helps university students prepare for examinations with CBT-style practice, study materials, an AI learning assistant and progress tracking. It is built first for students of the Federal University of Agriculture, Abeokuta (FUNAAB), with a vision of serving more institutions across Nigeria.

## Long biography
PRIME CBT was created to make examination preparation more organised and accessible. Many students revise from scattered notes and have few chances to practise in the format of a computer-based test. PRIME CBT brings practice, materials and feedback together: students practise by course and topic with a timer and instant feedback, open study materials and ask an AI assistant for simple explanations, and track accuracy, weak topics and study streaks. Features such as an exam countdown, focus sessions, community and a leaderboard help students stay consistent. The platform works in a mobile or desktop browser and can be installed on supported devices. PRIME CBT starts with FUNAAB students and aims to expand to universities, colleges and other institutions across Nigeria. [ADD: launch date, if you want to state it]
